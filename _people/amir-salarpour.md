    <h3>Biography</h3>
    <p>
      Dr. Amir Salarpour is currently a <strong>PostDoctoral Researcher at Clemson University (USA)</strong>, focusing on Computer Vision and Signal Processing. 
    </p>
    <p>
      Previously, as an Assistant Professor at <em>Sirjan University of Technology</em>, he was my direct supervisor for two years (Feb 2019 – Feb 2021). During this intense collaboration, I served as his <strong>Undergraduate Thesis Student</strong>, <strong>Research Assistant (RA)</strong>, and <strong>Teaching Assistant (TA)</strong>.
    </p>
    <p>
      Under his mentorship, I achieved a perfect score of <strong>20/20</strong> on my thesis titled <em>"Large-scale Semantic Point Cloud Segmentation"</em> and honed my skills in implementing complex Deep Learning algorithms entirely from scratch, prioritizing <strong>mathematical mastery</strong> over pre-built libraries.
    </p>
    
    <div style="background-color: #f0f7fb; border-left: 4px solid #2e86c1; padding: 15px; font-style: italic; color: #555; margin-top: 15px;">
