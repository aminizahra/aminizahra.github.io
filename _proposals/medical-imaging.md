## Background

Segmentation models often lose accuracy when data come from different scanners or sites, or when only a few annotated examples are available.

## Research questions

- How can segmentation stay dependable across data sources?
- How can models learn well from limited annotations?

## Objectives

- Study the robustness of segmentation across data sources
- Reduce the need for annotated data

## Approach

Deep learning segmentation with a focus on robustness and limited supervision.

## Related experience

- Medical image segmentation with U-Net
- 3D point cloud semantic segmentation (B.Sc. thesis)
