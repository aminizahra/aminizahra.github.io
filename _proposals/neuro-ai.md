## Background

Understanding how the visual cortex integrates information from both eyes is central to vision research, and neuroimaging and EEG signals make it possible to study this process computationally.

## Research questions

- How can generative models capture visual cortex activity from neuroimaging and EEG signals?
- How can their outputs be validated and made interpretable?

## Objectives

- Develop and evaluate multimodal generative models of visual cortex activity
- Quantify the uncertainty of the model outputs
- Assess the interpretability of the learned representations

## Approach

Generative and multimodal modelling with uncertainty estimation and explainability analysis, applied to neuroimaging and EEG signals.

## Related experience

- Earlier work on EEG-based epilepsy prediction
- Calibrated general anaesthesia triage model (manuscript under review)
- Time-series and bio-signal analysis
