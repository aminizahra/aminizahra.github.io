## Background

Clinical decision support is only useful if clinicians can trust the predicted probabilities and understand the reasoning behind a decision.

## Research questions

- How can prediction models give well-calibrated probabilities on clinical data?
- How can explanations be made reliable and useful to clinicians?

## Objectives

- Improve and evaluate the calibration of clinical prediction models
- Evaluate explanations for clinical transparency
- Assess performance beyond the development dataset

## Approach

Calibration methods and explainable AI techniques applied to clinical tabular data.
